RaiseWell Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

RaiseWell is developed by Weegix Technology Solutions LLC ("Weegix," "we," "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how RaiseWell handles information.

Summary

RaiseWell does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to any third party. All of your progress and activity in the app stays on your own device.

What RaiseWell Stores

RaiseWell stores your reading progress (which chapters you've marked as read) in a local database on your device only. This information:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never transmitted to Weegix or any third party
  • Is never used for advertising or analytics
  • Is deleted if you uninstall the app

What RaiseWell Does Not Do

  • RaiseWell does not require an account, login, or any personal information to use.
  • RaiseWell does not collect your name, email address, location, or device identifiers.
  • RaiseWell does not contain advertising or third-party trackers.
  • RaiseWell does not share any information with third parties, because none is collected.

In-App Purchases

If RaiseWell offers an in-app purchase in the future, that purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing (or Apple's App Store, on iOS). Weegix never sees or stores your payment card details; Google or Apple handles that transaction according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

RaiseWell is intended for use by parents and caregivers, not by children directly. RaiseWell is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
